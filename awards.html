Awards

Recognizing AI⁴Methane members whose work moves the network and the global methane budget forward.

Impact Award

Recognizes a member whose work has substantially advanced the network's mission of better constraining global methane sources and sinks, through scientific advances, new methods or data products, or leadership that brings the community together and strengthens collaboration across AI⁴Methane.

Futures Award

Recognizes a graduate student or early-career researcher whose leadership and contributions show exceptional promise for the future of methane science, such as leading collaborative analyses, synthesis papers, or tools that move the network's work forward.
